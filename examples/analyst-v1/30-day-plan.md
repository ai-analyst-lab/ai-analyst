# 30-day plan

- First use: one approved descriptive question.
- First user: name before pilot.
- Owner: course participant.
- Baseline: record current time, quality review, and decision path.
- Success measure: define before first use.
- Weekly review: inspect runs, corrections, failures, and user feedback.
- Stop condition: a blocking evaluation failure or policy violation.
- Day 30 decision: expand, revise, pause, or retire with evidence.
