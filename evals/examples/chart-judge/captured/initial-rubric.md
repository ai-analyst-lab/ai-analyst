# Captured chart judge rubric

Rubric version: captured-5-rule

The chart passes overall only if every rule passes.

## Action title

- Pass: The title states the takeaway with a direction or magnitude.
- Fail: The title only names the axes or subject.
- Unknown: The title is not visible.

## Focus color

- Pass: One deliberate accent color directs attention on a neutral background.
- Fail: Default colors or several competing colors provide no clear focus.
- Unknown: Color cannot be evaluated from the supplied artifact.

## Direct label

- Pass: The value the reader needs is labeled directly on the chart.
- Fail: The reader must estimate the important value from an axis.
- Unknown: Labels are not legible in the supplied artifact.

## Decluttered

- Pass: Borders, gridlines, markers, and labels support rather than compete with the message.
- Fail: Heavy gridlines, a full box border, repeated markers, or redundant labels add avoidable clutter.
- Unknown: The complete chart frame is not visible.

## Zero baseline

- Pass: A bar chart starts at zero, or a nonzero baseline is clearly justified for a chart form where it is appropriate.
- Fail: A truncated baseline exaggerates a comparison without clear disclosure or justification.
- Unknown: The scale or chart form cannot be determined.
